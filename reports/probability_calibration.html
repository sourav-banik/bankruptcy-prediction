Probability calibration diagnostics

Interpretation: The validation and test cohorts are constructed using matched bankrupt and non-bankrupt firms, so their observed prevalence is not a population bankruptcy rate. Treat model outputs and Platt-scaled outputs as relative bankruptcy risk scores, not true population probabilities. A representative validation sample or external prevalence information is needed for population calibration.

Calibration method

Platt scaling was fit using only the validation predictions and labels. The held-out test metrics use that fixed transformation. Validation Platt metrics are in-sample calibration diagnostics and are optimistic. No test data were used to fit the transformation.
